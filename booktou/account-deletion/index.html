Booktou

Delete your account

This page explains how to delete your Booktou Friend Streak account and the cloud data linked to it.

Email a deletion request

Delete your account in Booktou

  1. Open Profile, then Settings.
  2. Under Experimental Features, open Friend Streak Account.
  3. Select Delete Friend Streak Account and confirm your Google or Apple account.

Deletion starts immediately after successful confirmation.

If you cannot access the app

Email rapheldorsoftware@gmail.com from the address connected to your account. Include your sign-in provider and display name if known.

Never send us your password, verification code, or private key.

Deleted

  • Your Friend Streak sign-in account
  • Your pairing, invitations, and shared streak progress

Not affected

  • Your local library and reading history
  • Your personal Google Drive backups
  • Store purchases or active subscriptions

Processing time

In-app deletion is immediate. Email requests are normally completed within 30 days after account ownership is verified.

NoteToMe Notes in a chat style

Notes that feel like a chat.

NoteToMe is a modern notes app that lets you write notes as if you were messaging yourself. Instead of long lists, every note is a conversation, so capturing and managing notes is faster and more natural. Works offline and keeps your data on your device.

Coming soon to the App Store

What you can do

Chat-based notes

Every note is a conversation. Customise the chat name, colour, background and emoji.

Groups and unified feeds

Bring several chats together and read notes from different chats in one combined feed.

Rich content

Add photos, videos, voice notes, files, locations and contacts.

Star what matters

Mark messages as starred and open all starred notes from one place.

Trash management

Restore deleted notes, delete selected items permanently or empty the trash in one action.

Clean, modern design

A minimal, distraction-free interface with light, dark, system and high-contrast dark themes.

Privacy and security

App lock with PIN or biometrics, encrypted backups, and your data stays on your device.

Backup and restore

Create a single encrypted backup file and restore your notes easily when you switch devices.

Personalisation

Adjustable font size, 12h / 24h time format, custom chat colours and backgrounds.

Privacy first

NoteToMe works offline and needs no internet connection. Your notes stay on your device and are never shared with third parties.

NoteToMe Notes in a chat style

Notes that feel like a chat.

Write notes as if you were messaging yourself. Customise chats, group them into one unified feed, attach photos, videos, voice notes, files, locations and contacts, star what matters, and keep everything private with an app lock and encrypted backups.

Coming soon

Store screenshots. Appearance may vary by version and language.

What you can do

Chat-based notes

Write notes in separate conversations. Each chat can have its own colour, background and custom order.

Groups and unified feeds

Group related chats together and browse all notes across a group in a single unified feed.

Rich content

Attach photos, videos, voice notes, files, map locations and contact cards to any note.

Star what matters

Star important notes and browse them quickly in one place or inside any chat.

Trash management

Deleted notes, chats and groups move to trash where you can restore them or delete them permanently.

Clean, modern design

Choose between auto, light, dark and high-contrast dark themes.

Privacy and security

App lock with PIN or biometrics, encrypted backups, and your data stays on your device.

Backup and restore

Create a single encrypted backup file and restore your notes easily when you switch devices.

Personalisation

Adjustable font size, 12h / 24h time format, custom chat colours and backgrounds.

Privacy first

NoteToMe works offline and needs no internet connection. Your notes stay on your device and are never shared with third parties.

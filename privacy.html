Website privacy policy

This document covers the website. Each app has separate policies.

Website policyApp policies

Last updated: 6 November 2026

1. Scope and contact

This privacy policy covers the Rapheldor Software website at rapheldorsoftware.com and www.rapheldorsoftware.com. Rapheldor Software is operated by Efe Kırbız in Türkiye. For questions or privacy requests, email rapheldorsoftware@gmail.com.

This policy does not describe data processing inside our mobile apps or games. Each product has its own privacy policy, available in the app policies section below.

2. Website hosting and technical information

The website is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. Your browser sends technical information needed to request pages and assets. GitHub states that visitors' IP addresses are logged and stored for security purposes, whether or not a visitor is signed in to GitHub. Hosting may involve processing outside your country.

See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub General Privacy Statement for the hosting provider's practices, retention and safeguards. We do not set GitHub's log-retention periods.

3. Cookies, language preferences and tracking

The website's own code does not set cookies or add advertising, analytics trackers or third-party video embeds. Fonts, screenshots and other website assets are served from this site.

To remember a language you select, the site stores a language code in your browser's local storage under rapheldor-language. This preference is not an account or an advertising identifier and is not sent to an analytics service. It remains until you change your selection or clear the site's browser data. The unlocalized home page may also use your browser's preferred languages to choose a language. Explicit localized page URLs take priority.

You can clear this preference through your browser's site-data settings. If browser storage is unavailable, you can still browse the website and use language links.

4. Email correspondence

The website has no registration, checkout or contact form. If you email us, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address, name, message and attachments, along with information normally included in email delivery. We use it to answer your request, provide support and keep necessary correspondence records. Our email provider processes messages to deliver and store them.

Do not send passwords, payment details or identity documents. We keep correspondence only as long as needed for the request and any applicable recordkeeping, dispute-resolution or legal obligations. We do not publish support correspondence or use it to build advertising profiles.

5. Purposes and legal grounds

We use information to operate and secure the website, remember your language choice and respond to messages. Where applicable law requires a legal basis, relevant grounds may include our legitimate interests in providing a secure website and responding to enquiries, steps you request before entering an agreement, and compliance with legal obligations. We do not sell your personal information.

6. External links

App Store, Google Play and other external links open services operated by third parties. Those services receive information when you visit them and apply their own privacy terms. Following an email link opens your configured email application.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion or a copy of personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. Contact us using the email above and describe your request; do not include sensitive identity documents. Rights may be subject to lawful exceptions. You may also contact your relevant data-protection authority. For information controlled by GitHub or another external provider, consult that provider's privacy statement and request channels.

8. Changes

We may update this policy when the website or its data practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision. The website's terms of use apply separately.

Quick answers

What can you do with Speaktou?

Speaktou brings speaking practice, vocabulary, and progress together in one short daily session.

How does Speaktou score my pronunciation?

Speaktou listens to your voice with real speech recognition and scores what you said against the target phrase, word by word, right after you speak.

Can I hear the correct pronunciation first?

Yes. Every card has a native text-to-speech playback button, so you can hear a word or phrase said correctly before — or after — you try it yourself.

What is the Word Tower?

It's your personal, growing collection of vocabulary. Every new word you learn is added as a brick, and a Word of the Day keeps it growing.

Do I need an internet connection?

Sessions are short and offline-friendly, so you can fit practice into any busy day.

When is Speaktou launching, and will it be free?

Speaktou is launching soon for iPhone and Android. It will be free to download, with an optional Premium upgrade for unlimited practice and deeper insights.

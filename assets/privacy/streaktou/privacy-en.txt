# Streaktou Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 6, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how Nurullah KIRBIZ, an independent software developer based in Türkiye and publishing under the name Rapheldor Software ("we", "us", or "our"), handles information when you use the Streaktou mobile application (the "App") and the Streaktou pages on rapheldorsoftware.com.

## 1. At a glance

Streaktou stores your challenges, sessions, notes and proof photos locally on your device. There is no Streaktou account and no server of ours that receives your habit data. Optional backups go to your own cloud storage.

The free version of the App may show ads through Google AdMob and Unity Ads, including optional rewarded ads. Streaktou Plus removes ads. RevenueCat helps us validate and restore Plus purchases. These providers process the limited information described in sections 6 and 7. The App does not send your habit names, notes, sessions or proof photos to them. We do not sell your personal information.

## 2. Information stored on your device

The App stores the following only in its private storage on your device:

- your challenges, including their names, durations, days and reminder times;
- completed sessions, dates, session lengths, notes and streak information, including streak freezes;
- proof photos you take or choose; and
- settings such as language, appearance, color theme, haptics and backup preferences.

We cannot see or access this information. Uninstalling the App removes it from your device, subject to your operating system's own device backup behavior.

## 3. Camera and photo library

The camera and photo library are used only when you take or choose a proof photo. The selected photo is copied into the App's private storage. We never upload your photos, and they are never sent to advertising or purchase providers.

## 4. Notifications, widgets and Live Activities

Reminders and session notifications are scheduled locally on your device. Home Screen widgets, Lock Screen widgets and Live Activities show your streak and session timer using data shared only between the App and its widget extension on the same device. No notification or widget data leaves your device.

## 5. Backups to your own cloud

Backup is optional and off by default. If you turn it on:

- **iCloud (iOS):** an archive of your data is saved to the App's folder in your own iCloud Drive. Apple's privacy policy applies.
- **Google Drive:** an archive is saved to a hidden, app-specific folder in your own Google Drive. Google Sign-In is used only to request permission for that folder. Google's privacy policy applies.
- **Backup file:** you can export an archive and save it wherever you choose.

We never receive your backups, your Apple or Google credentials, or your access tokens.

## 6. Purchases

Streaktou Plus is sold through the App Store and Google Play. Apple or Google processes payments; we do not receive your payment card details.

RevenueCat processes an anonymous app user identifier, purchase and subscription records, store transaction identifiers, entitlement status, app and device information, and connection information such as IP address. This is used to validate purchases, restore access and manage subscription status. When a subscription expires or a purchase is refunded, Plus access may be removed. RevenueCat's privacy policy: [https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy).

## 7. Advertising and your privacy choices

The free version may show ads, including optional rewarded ads: watching an eligible rewarded ad to the end can grant a streak freeze; ad availability and daily limits apply. Streaktou Plus users are not shown ads.

Google AdMob and Unity Ads may process device and advertising identifiers, IP address and approximate location derived from it, device and app information, ad interactions and diagnostics. They use this to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and respect your privacy choices. The App does not request GPS location permission.

Personalized advertising depends on your consent and device settings. Where required by law, a consent form appears before any ad is requested, and you can revisit your choices at any time in **Profile > Ad privacy choices**. On iOS, you can decline tracking in the system prompt or later in Settings. Declining tracking or personalized ads does not limit the App's core features.

Provider policies: Google [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and Unity [https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy](https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy).

## 8. Children

Streaktou is not directed to children under 13. Advertising and purchase providers have their own privacy practices. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us at the address below.

## 9. Your choices and data deletion

You can edit or delete your notes, turn off reminders, disconnect backups, change your ad privacy choices and delete the App at any time. Deleting the App removes your local data, subject to operating system backups. Backups can be deleted from your iCloud or Google Drive storage settings. Purchase records held by Apple, Google or RevenueCat may be kept for billing, fraud prevention and legal obligations; contact us for requests about purchase-related data.

## 10. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date shows the latest version. Material changes will be reflected on this page.

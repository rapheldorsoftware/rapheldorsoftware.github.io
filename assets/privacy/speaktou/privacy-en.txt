# Speaktou Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 16, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how Nurullah KIRBIZ, an independent software developer based in Türkiye and publishing under the name Rapheldor Software ("we", "us", or "our"), handles information when you use the Speaktou mobile application (the "App") and the Speaktou pages on rapheldorsoftware.com.

## 1. At a glance

Speaktou is local-first. Your practice history, streaks, accuracy scores, weak-word list, collected vocabulary ("Word Tower"), and most settings are stored on your device. We do not upload this content to our servers as part of the App's core, offline practice loop.

Cloud processing occurs when you sign in and we synchronize your progress so it isn't lost if you change devices, and also through the Apple/Google speech-recognition and text-to-speech services the App relies on to score your pronunciation. The sections below explain each case.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your practice attempts, recordings, or vocabulary history to build advertising profiles, and Speaktou does not show advertisements.

## 2. Information stored locally

The following information may be stored on your device:

- practice cards and daily words shown to you, your spoken-attempt transcripts and accuracy scores, streaks, accuracy history, weak-word tracking, and your collected Word Tower entries;
- your daily practice goal, reminder times, app language, and appearance settings;
- purchase status and technical records needed to restore or validate Premium access; and
- basic connection state used to decide when to sync.

You control this local data. Uninstalling the App generally removes app data, subject to your operating system's backup and restore behavior. Because local data can be lost through uninstallation, device failure, operating-system changes, or software errors, you should not treat the device as your only record of progress you care about — this is also why the optional account sync in the next section exists.

## 3. Account, sign-in, and cloud sync

You can use Speaktou as a guest, or sign in with Google or Apple through Firebase Authentication to keep your streak and progress if you switch devices or reinstall the App. iOS offers both Google and Apple sign-in; Android currently offers Google sign-in.

When you sign in, we and our service providers may process:

- a Firebase user identifier and your selected sign-in provider;
- the email address, display name, and profile photo returned by Google or Apple for authentication (Apple may provide a private relay address); and
- your practice attempts, streaks, accuracy history, weak-word tracking, and Word Tower entries, synchronized to Cloud Firestore under access rules that restrict each account's data to that account only.

We use this information to authenticate you, keep your progress available across devices, provide account deletion, and keep the feature secure. Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore are provided by Google Firebase. Sign-in is provided by Google or Apple depending on your choice. If you use the App as a guest, none of this section applies until you choose to sign in.

### App Check and device integrity

Speaktou uses Firebase App Check to help verify that Firebase requests come from a genuine copy of the App. On Android this uses Google Play Integrity; on Apple platforms it uses Apple DeviceCheck. These providers may process app, device, network, and integrity signals and return an attestation result. Speaktou receives the validation result rather than the underlying device-attestation details.

### Account retention and deletion

Account and cloud practice records are retained while your account is active or as needed to operate the feature. You can permanently delete your account from **Settings > Delete account**, which requires re-authenticating with your sign-in provider. This deletes your Firebase authentication account and your cloud practice data. Deleting your account does **not** cancel an active Google Play or App Store subscription — cancel it separately in the relevant store before or after deleting your account.

## 4. Microphone and speech recognition

When you tap the microphone to practice, Speaktou uses your device's built-in speech-recognition service (Apple's Speech framework on iOS, or the Android platform's speech recognizer) to convert what you said into text, which the App then scores against the target phrase. Speech recognition is provided by the operating system, not by Speaktou's own servers; depending on your device and language settings, this may require an active internet connection and may involve audio being processed by Apple's or Google's speech-recognition service under their own privacy terms. Speaktou does not record, store, or transmit your raw voice audio to our own servers, and we do not keep an audio file of your practice attempts — only the resulting transcript and accuracy score, handled as described in Sections 2 and 3.

Microphone access is requested only for this pronunciation-practice feature and is used while you are actively practicing.

## 5. Text-to-speech playback

Speaktou plays the correct pronunciation of practice phrases and words using your device's built-in text-to-speech engine. This runs entirely on-device; no data is collected or transmitted as part of this feature.

## 6. Notifications

Speaktou can schedule two kinds of local reminders — a daily practice reminder and a word-of-the-day reminder — at times you choose. These are scheduled directly on your device using its local notification system; Speaktou does not use push notifications or send notification content through our servers.

## 7. Purchases and subscriptions

Speaktou Premium is offered as an auto-renewing subscription through Google Play or Apple's App Store. The store processes payment information, transaction identifiers, subscription status, and account information under its own policies. Rapheldor Software does not receive your full payment-card details. Speaktou receives and stores the purchase status and transaction information needed to unlock, restore, and manage Premium access.

## 8. Website data

The Speaktou legal pages are static pages hosted through GitHub Pages. We do not place our own analytics cookies on these pages. The hosting provider may process IP addresses and technical request logs for security and service operation under its own policy. If you contact us by email, we process your email address and message to answer the request and, when applicable, verify and complete account deletion.

## 9. Legal bases and international processing

Where data-protection law requires a legal basis, we process information as necessary to provide features you request and perform our agreement with you; based on your consent for optional sign-in where applicable; and for legitimate interests such as service security, abuse prevention, troubleshooting, and legal compliance. You can withdraw optional permissions through the App, device settings, provider account settings, or by deleting your account.

Firebase, Google, Apple, and GitHub may process information in countries other than your own. Their privacy terms and applicable safeguards govern their processing.

## 10. Data sharing

We disclose information only as described in this policy:

- to service providers necessary to supply authentication, cloud synchronization, integrity checks, speech recognition/text-to-speech, purchases, and website hosting;
- when required by law or a valid legal request; or
- to protect users, the App, our rights, or the public from fraud, abuse, or security threats.

We do not sell or rent personal information.

## 11. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures, including authenticated Firebase access rules limiting each account to its own data, App Check, platform sign-in, and encrypted network connections. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Keep your device and provider accounts protected.

## 12. Children

Speaktou is not directed to children under 13, and accounts are not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from a child under 13 without authorization required by applicable law. A parent or guardian who believes a child provided account information can contact us to request review and deletion.

## 13. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or receive a copy of personal information and to withdraw consent. Most Speaktou content is local and is not accessible to us. For your cloud account data, use the in-app deletion control described in Section 3, or contact us for help.

You can manage:

- local data by uninstalling the App;
- your account and cloud sync in Speaktou's Settings;
- microphone and notification permissions in device settings; and
- subscriptions through Google Play or the App Store.

If applicable, you may lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority. Users in Türkiye may also have the rights provided under Article 11 of Turkish Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data (KVKK).

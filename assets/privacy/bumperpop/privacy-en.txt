 Bumper Pop! Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 15, 2026

Last Updated: October 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Nurullah KIRBIZ, an independent software developer
based in Türkiye and publishing under the name Rapheldor Software ("Rapheldor,"
"we," "us," or "our"), handles information in connection with the mobile puzzle game
Bumper Pop! (the "Game") for Android and iOS.

 1. Privacy at a Glance

Bumper Pop! is a free match-3 puzzle game. It is funded by advertising. The current version:

- stores your game progress and settings only on your device;
- shows ads provided by Google AdMob: full-screen ads at natural breaks (for example when you restart a level) and optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch for extra moves;
- uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), and lets you change your choice later from the Settings screen;
- does not offer in-app purchases;
- does not require or offer an account or sign-in; and
- does not request access to contacts, photos, the camera, the microphone or location.

Rapheldor itself does not receive, sell or share personal information collected by the ad services described in Section 3.

 2. Information Stored Only on Your Device

The Game stores a limited amount of information locally on your device so it can remember your progress and preferences:

- level stars and best scores, coins, boosters and unlocked garage paints;
- daily round progress and a level you have left unfinished;
- selected language;
- music, sound, vibration, accessibility and animation settings;
- which tutorials and tips you have already seen; and
- the date on which you accepted this Privacy Policy and the Terms of Use.

This information never leaves your device through the Game. It remains on your device until you clear the Game's app data or uninstall the Game. Device backup and restore features provided by your operating system or app store may process local app data in accordance with their own terms and privacy policies.

 3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob) to show ads, and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and store your privacy choices. When ads are requested, Google and its advertising partners may collect and process:

- on Android, your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID) and other device identifiers; on iOS, identifiers other than Apple's advertising identifier (see below);
- IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
- device and app information, such as device model, operating system, language, app version and screen size;
- ad interaction data, such as ad impressions, clicks and video completion; and
- diagnostic information used to measure, debug and secure ad delivery and to prevent fraud.

This information is used to deliver, measure and limit the frequency of ads, to detect fraud and abuse and, where you consent or where the law allows, to show personalized ads. In regions where consent is required, you are asked for your choice before personalized ads are shown; you can change your choice at any time with the "Privacy choices" button in the Game's Settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in your device settings (Android: Settings > Google > Ads or Settings > Privacy > Ads; iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking and Apple Advertising).

On iOS, the Game does not ask for permission to track you through Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework, so Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) is not accessed and ads are never personalized using it. The Google Mobile Ads SDK on iOS is configured not to start until the Game starts it; the iOS version shows ads only where advertising has been enabled for iOS, and it does not collect any data before that.

Ads in the Game are requested with a maximum content rating suitable for general audiences with parental guidance (PG). Rapheldor does not receive the identifiers listed above. Google's processing is governed by Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and its explanation of how Google uses information from apps that use its services (https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

 4. Voluntary Support and Bug Reports

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address, the contents of your message and anything you choose to attach. We use this information only to respond to you, investigate and fix problems, protect our support channel from abuse and comply with applicable legal obligations. Please do not include passwords, government-issued identification numbers, financial information or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Our support email is hosted by a third-party email provider, currently Google's Gmail service.

 5. App Store and Platform Information

Google Play, the Apple App Store, Android, iOS, device manufacturers and operating system services may independently process information such as downloads, installations, device details, app store account information and crash or performance diagnostics. Their processing is governed by their own privacy policies.

Rapheldor may receive aggregated or pseudonymized reports made available through Google Play or the Apple App Store, such as the Game version, device model, operating system version, crash stack traces and installation statistics. We use these reports solely to maintain and improve the Game.

The Game is built with the Unity engine. The Game does not enable Unity's optional analytics or advertising services; ads are provided only by Google AdMob as described in Section 3.

 6. Legal Bases, Retention and Your Rights

Where data protection laws such as the GDPR, the UK GDPR or the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK) apply, we rely on our legitimate interests in operating, securing and improving the Game, on your consent for personalized advertising where consent is required, and on compliance with legal obligations.

We keep support correspondence only as long as needed to handle the request and to meet legal obligations. Data handled by the app stores and platform providers is retained by those providers under their own policies.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal information, to object to processing and to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority. To exercise these rights for information we hold, contact us at rapheldorsoftware@gmail.com.

Data deletion requests for Bumper Pop!

To delete progress and preferences stored on your device, clear the Game's app data or uninstall the Game. Advertising data is held by Google, not by Rapheldor: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings and manage your Google data at https://myaccount.google.com. To request deletion of support correspondence held by Rapheldor, email rapheldorsoftware@gmail.com with the subject "Bumper Pop data deletion" and identify the correspondence you want deleted. Do not send passwords or identity documents. We delete correspondence we no longer need, except records that must be retained to meet a legal obligation or resolve an active dispute; any applicable reason and retention period will be explained in our response.

 7. Security

Information stored on your device is protected by your device's own security. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

 8. Children

Bumper Pop! is a casual game for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. Rapheldor does not knowingly collect personal information from children, and ads are requested with a PG maximum content rating. Children should ask a parent or legal guardian before sending us an email. If you are a parent or legal guardian and believe that a child has sent us personal information, please contact us at rapheldorsoftware@gmail.com and we will delete it.

 9. Third-Party Links

The Game may open external links, including our website, this Privacy Policy, the Terms of Use, app store pages and your email application. We do not control the privacy practices or content of third-party services.

 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the Game, applicable laws or our privacy practices. We will update the "Last Updated" date and, where required, ask you to review and accept the updated policy inside the Game or provide notice through the relevant app store.

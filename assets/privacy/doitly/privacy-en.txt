Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 4, 2026

Doitly ("App") stores tasks and settings locally. Versions that include advertising use Google AdMob, which may process device and usage information as described below.

1. Personal Information

The App does not request personal information such as name, email, or photos.

2. To-Dos

All to-dos created by the user are stored only on the device.
This data is not transmitted to the internet, servers, or third parties.

3. Data Usage

Data is used solely to provide the core functionality of managing to-dos.
Your task text is not intentionally provided to advertisers. AdMob may process advertising/device identifiers, IP addresses, ad interactions, app activity and diagnostics for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Advertising availability and personalization depend on the installed version, device privacy settings and applicable consent choices.

[Google privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

If you contact support, we receive the address, message and attachments you choose to send and use them to respond to your request.

4. Data Deletion

Users can delete all to-dos at any time using the “delete data” option.
Uninstalling the App will permanently remove all data from the device.
Backups created by the user are outside the App’s control and must be deleted manually.

5. Security

Task data remains local. Advertising and support services may transfer information over the internet.
100% security cannot be guaranteed; users are responsible for their device security.

6. Third-Party Libraries

Third-party services process information under their applicable privacy policies. Clearing task data does not necessarily delete advertising information already received by providers. Contact rapheldorsoftware@gmail.com for applicable privacy requests, including access, correction or deletion. We keep support information only as long as needed to respond, resolve issues and meet legal obligations. Service-provider records follow the relevant retention settings and policies.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 4, 2026

Dictiony ("App") stores practice records and settings locally. Network services may process information for speech recognition, advertising, purchases and usage analytics, as described below. Availability depends on the installed version and platform.

1. Personal Information

The App does not request personal information such as name, email, or photos.

2. User Data

All data created by the user is stored only on the device.
Raw practice text and recognised speech are not intentionally sent to Firebase Analytics. Selected exercise identifiers and performance metrics may be processed as described in the Analytics section below.

3. Speech Recognition

The App uses the device’s built-in speech recognition services.
The operating system speech recogniser may process audio on the device or through its provider’s online service, depending on the device, language and recogniser settings. Microphone permission is required for speaking practice. Rapheldor does not operate its own speech-recognition server. Refer to Google or Apple privacy information for the recogniser used by your device.

4. Advertising

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob and its partners may automatically collect device and usage information (for example: advertising identifiers, device model, OS version, and ad interaction data) to serve and measure ads. We do not use your speech recordings, practice content, or other user-created content for advertising, profiling, or marketing purposes.

Third-party services used:

Google AdMob policies/support — https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543?hl=en

5. Premium Subscription

Dictiony offers a Premium Subscription via in-app purchases on Google Play. The available plan, billing period and renewal terms are shown by the store before purchase. Subscriptions automatically renew unless cancelled through the store. Purchasing the Premium Subscription removes advertisements within the App and may provide additional premium features or functionality.

All subscription payments are processed by Google Play. Payment details are handled by Google and are not shared with Dictiony. For refunds, subscription management, or cancellation, please use Google Play > Subscriptions or visit: https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions

6. Data Usage

Data is used solely to provide the core functionality of the App.
We do not send raw spoken transcripts or user-authored practice content to advertisers. Firebase Analytics may receive practice identifiers, durations, completion and accuracy metrics, streak milestones, screen views, game scores, purchase attempts and ad interactions to help us understand feature usage. It may also process app-instance identifiers, device/app details and general location derived from IP addresses. Collection depends on the build and service settings.

[Firebase privacy information](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)
[Google privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
[Apple privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)

7. Data Deletion

Users can delete all data at any time using the in-app reset option.
Uninstalling the App removes local app data, subject to operating-system backup and restore behavior. Analytics, ad and transaction records already received by service providers are subject to their retention settings and policies.
Backups created by the user are outside the App's control and must be deleted manually.

8. Security

Local practice records remain on the device, while the services described above may transfer information over the internet.
100% security cannot be guaranteed; users are responsible for their device security.

9. Third-Party Libraries

The App uses service providers for the functions described above. These providers process information under their applicable privacy policies and service terms. Contact rapheldorsoftware@gmail.com for requests concerning data processed on our behalf. Clearing app data does not necessarily delete information already received by third-party services. Applicable access, correction, deletion and objection rights depend on your jurisdiction.

10. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 13 without parental consent, we will take steps to delete that information. Parents or guardians who believe their child under 13 has provided personal information may contact us at rapheldorsoftware@gmail.com to request deletion.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes are made, we will update the "Last Updated" date. Significant changes will be highlighted or communicated in-app where feasible.

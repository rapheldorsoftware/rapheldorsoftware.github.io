Sleeptou Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Sleeptou is a bedtime wind-down companion provided by Rapheldor Software. This policy describes the planned release with RevenueCat subscription management.

Your bedtime data

Ritual settings, sessions, routine history, morning reflections, self-reported sleep hours and notes are stored on your device. We do not upload this information to our servers or RevenueCat. Sleeptou does not require an account and does not measure sleep or provide medical advice.

Premium subscriptions and RevenueCat

Apple App Store or Google Play processes payments. We do not receive your payment-card details. RevenueCat processes purchase history, store transaction and receipt information, subscription status, product identifiers and an automatically generated anonymous app user identifier to validate purchases, restore access, prevent fraud and manage Premium entitlements. Its subscription dashboard also provides purchase analytics. Subscription requests can include technical information needed to communicate with the service, such as app and SDK versions, platform, locale and currency.

We plan to use anonymous RevenueCat identifiers, without sending names, email addresses, bedtime notes or advertising identifiers to RevenueCat. We do not use advertising tracking or sell personal information. See RevenueCat's privacy policy, Apple's privacy policy and Google's privacy policy.

Notifications, widgets and backups

Optional notifications remind you to wind down. Background audio, widgets and Live Activities display your chosen session information. Android automatic cloud backup is disabled for app data. iPhone device backups may include app data depending on your device settings. If you export a backup or share it, you choose its destination and the destination's privacy rules apply.

Support

If you contact support, we receive the email address and information you choose to send. Avoid sending private bedtime notes or payment-card details. We use support correspondence to answer your request.

Retention, deletion and your choices

Local app data remains until you delete it using the app's reset controls or remove the app; exported files and device backups must be managed separately. Purchase records are retained as needed to provide subscriptions and meet applicable obligations. To request deletion of data processed for subscription management, contact rapheldorsoftware@gmail.com. We may need information sufficient to locate the anonymous purchase record. Do not send passwords or full payment details. Data deletion does not cancel a subscription; manage or cancel it in the store.

Security and international processing

RevenueCat encrypts subscription data in transit. Service providers may process data outside your country. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Updates and contact

We may update this policy as the app changes. Contact Rapheldor Software at rapheldorsoftware@gmail.com for privacy questions.
